About the Club

Vandalia Chess Club brings local players together every week, from first-timers learning how the knight moves to rated players chasing their next milestone.

The club was started by DeVante Bush, who reached a peak chess.com rapid rating of 1490 in March 2024 and earned his best win against a 1450-rated opponent.
